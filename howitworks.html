How It Works

Sign Up & Explore

Create an account and explore available farming projects,
whether you’re an investor or a farmer seeking capital.

Choose & Invest

Investors select their preferred farming project and provide capital.
Farmers submit proposals to receive funding for their farms.

Harvest & Profit

Upon successful harvesting, profits are shared with investors based on their contributions,
while farmers enjoy the capital growth of their farms.

Grow & Monitor

Our team ensures farming processes are managed effectively,
with progress reports shared throughout the farming season.
